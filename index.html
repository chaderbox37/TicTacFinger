Welcome to Tic Tac Finger

Defeat your opponent using logic on a 5x5 grid.

STANDARD WIN: Be the first to get 5 pieces in a row horizontally, vertically, or diagonally.

SECRET WINS: Catch your opponent off guard with non-linear unblockable setups that use 5 or 6 pieces.
